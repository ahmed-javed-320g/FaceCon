On the data. Our real controlled-access data is still pending, so on our supervisor's advice we built synthetic data that exercises the whole pipeline now — and crucially, the real-data loader is the only thing that changes when access lands. How it is generated: ancestry-structured genotypes as 0/1/2 dosages; a planted genotype-to-face mapping with variance split twenty percent genetic, thirty-five demographic, forty-five noise; and, for appearance, the HIrisPlex-S marker SNPs that the integrated forensic predictor reads to return eye, hair and skin colour — we integrate that external method rather than training a colour model of our own. The scale is dataset-worthy: 5,000 subjects by 5,000 SNPs, plus the HIrisPlex-S marker SNPs, faces of nearly 27,000 vertices, standard QC and preprocessing, and strictly subject-level splits.
