Appearance, and this is where our design is deliberately honest. The key point: FaceCon does not train a new model for colour. The same DNA feeds two branches. The shape branch is ours — genotype through facial SNP selection and the FaceCon models to FLAME parameters and a 3D mesh. The appearance branch is an integration: we take the same genotype, pull out the HIrisPlex-S marker SNPs, and hand them to HIrisPlex-S, the established forensic standard for eye, hair, and skin colour, which returns those three colours for the visualization. On the right you see skin and eye colour rendered on the predicted face. Because HIrisPlex-S is already validated and published, we report it as-is and we do not invent an accuracy on our own data — the 3DFN resource has no eye, hair, or skin colour labels to test against. We also keep two colour modes separate: the pigmentation colours on the face, and the green-to-red heatmap that shows reconstruction error by region.
