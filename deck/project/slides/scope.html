Scope

What is, and is not, part of the project

In scope

  • Reproducible genotype to FLAME 3D-shape pipeline on documented data
  • Appearance colour via integrated HIrisPlex-S: eye, hair, skin colour
  • Confounder-aware evaluation against demographic baselines
  • Interactive demonstrator and visualization

Out of scope

  • Photorealistic skin texture or full photoreal rendering
  • Age estimation (needs DNA methylation, not genotype)
  • Operational forensic identification
  • Any claim that DNA uniquely reconstructs an exact face

Scope, stated plainly. In scope: the reproducible shape pipeline, eye, hair and skin colour through the integrated HIrisPlex-S forensic method, the confounder-aware shape evaluation, and the demonstrator. Deliberately out of scope: photorealistic skin texture, age from DNA — which needs methylation data we do not have — operational forensic identification, and any claim that DNA pins down an exact face. Being explicit about what we do not claim is central to the project's honesty.
