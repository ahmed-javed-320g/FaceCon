FaceCon · F26-082

5

A quick tour of the related work. Difface showed deep learning can map SNPs to a 3D mesh, but on private data and not reproducibly. Facial-shape GWAS studies — White, Xiong, Claes — give us the genetic loci that justify an informed SNP panel. HIrisPlex-S is the forensic standard for predicting eye, hair and skin colour; that is the external method we integrate for appearance. And the confounding critique is what pushes us toward a confounder-aware evaluation. FaceCon's contribution is the open, documented, demographically-controlled baseline none of these provide together.
