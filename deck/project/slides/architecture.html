The system is a layered, modular pipeline, and the diagram shows the data flow through it. One genotype feeds two branches. The shape branch — facial SNP selection, the FaceCon models, FLAME parameters, and the predicted 3D mesh — is what we train. The appearance branch — HIrisPlex-S marker extraction and the external HIrisPlex-S predictor returning eye, hair, and skin colour — is integrated, not trained. Both converge at the rendered face. The design requirements that matter: strictly subject-level splits with no leakage, full reproducibility from a config and seed, interchangeable synthetic and real data loaders — that is our swap point — and one shared FLAME contract so every model is comparable. The constraints we work under: small paired samples, sensitive controlled-access data, ancestry confounding, and a decimated mesh for smooth browser rendering.
